01 / Why we existBulgaria • Albania • The Balkans

New places can be lonely. No one should walk alone.

After years serving in Bulgaria, we've met families beginning again far from home and churches doing faithful work with limited resources. The Bridge Ministries was born from those relationships and a simple conviction: where there is a gap, we can help build a bridge.
